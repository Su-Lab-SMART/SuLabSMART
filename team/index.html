People

Chih-Ying Su

Principal Investigator

chihyingsu@gmail.com · suchihying@smart.org.cn

Chih-Ying Su is a Senior Principal Investigator at Shenzhen Medical Academy of Research and Translation (SMART). Prior to this, she was a Professor in the Department of Neurobiology at UC San Diego. She completed her B.A. in Zoology at National Taiwan University and her Ph.D. in Neuroscience at Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine, then pursued postdoctoral research at Yale University.

r8ng@ucsd.edu
