Chih-Ying Su is a Senior Principal Investigator at the Shenzhen Medical Academy of Research and Translation (SMART). Before joining SMART, she was a Professor in the Department of Neurobiology at the University of California San Diego. She earned a B.A. in Zoology from National Taiwan University and a Ph.D. in Neuroscience from Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine, then completed postdoctoral research at Yale University.
